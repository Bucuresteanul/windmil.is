# Decision Dashboard

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-08  
**Decision:** CONDITIONAL CONTINUE — RESEARCH / MARKET ENTRY

This dashboard is intentionally short. Detailed evidence lives in the linked project files.

---

## Market Reality

**Status:** PASS

Iceland now has utility-scale wind in operation at Vaðalda.

This establishes a real construction and operating market, not merely theoretical resource potential.

---

## Immediate Open Package

**Status:** NOT ESTABLISHED

No public evidence reviewed to date establishes an immediately open foundations / erection package suitable for windmil.is.

This is the most important missing commercial fact.

---

## Nearer-Horizon Project

**Garpsdalur — EM Orka / Greenvolt Power**

**Status:** WATCH CLOSELY / REGULATORY GATE

Current developer material describes an 88.2 MW, 21-turbine project.

The energy minister proposed utilisation-category treatment in 2025, but 2026 public records still show unresolved Framework Programme treatment and white-tailed-eagle concerns.

**Trigger:** confirmed regulatory release + procurement architecture.

---

## Portfolio Relationship Target

**wpd Ísland**

**Status:** HIGH STRATEGIC RELEVANCE / EARLY

wpd has the strongest visible multi-project Icelandic portfolio identified so far.

The Brekknaheiði / Sauðanesháls cluster alone is published at up to 532.8 MW and 74 turbines, but remains far from proven bid readiness.

**Trigger:** framework / environmental / grid milestone or procurement signal.

## Procurement Precedent

**Status:** POSITIVE SIGNAL

The Vaðalda structures tender received seven bids and demonstrates both consortium and foreign-corporate participation.

Carbon cost was explicitly present in published tender comparisons.

This materially weakens the assumption that market access necessarily requires a fully Icelandic standalone contractor from day one.

See [Vaðalda Procurement Benchmark](./VADALDA-PROCUREMENT-BENCHMARK.md).

---

## Qualification

**Status:** PARTIAL — R1

Public rules now establish a credible path for a foreign EEA/EFTA service provider, but bid readiness still depends on package-specific requirements.

Key unresolved items:
- prequalification criteria
- insurance / bonds
- corporate / tax operating model
- regulated responsible roles
- machinery-right recognition
- reference thresholds
- customer-specific onboarding

See [Qualification & Access Gate](./QUALIFICATION-AND-ACCESS.md).

---

## Regulatory

**Status:** MATERIAL GATE

Law no. 66/2026 makes framework treatment explicit for wind projects at 10 MW+ **or** structures above 30 metres.

Municipal position, grid fit, regional value, birds, residents, wilderness and landscape are all material screening factors.

---

## Current Decision Logic

Continue only while work can materially answer one of these:

1. **Where is the first open or upcoming package?**
2. **Who controls access to it?**
3. **Can we qualify in time?**
4. **Can we deliver at acceptable risk and margin?**

Everything else is secondary.

---

## Next Evidence Threshold

Move from **market research** toward **commercial preparation** only when at least one target has:

**credible project maturity + identifiable package + probable procurement route + achievable qualification path**

Until then, avoid mobilisation costs and avoid pretending that pipeline MW equals addressable revenue.
