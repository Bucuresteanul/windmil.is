# Research Method

## Objective

Produce research that can change or justify a real market-entry decision.

This is not a general report on wind energy.

## Evidence Hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Primary

Official Icelandic government, regulator, grid, planning, environmental, municipal, project-owner and OEM sources.

### Tier 2 — Institutional and technical

European institutions, international energy bodies, academic research and recognized technical publications.

### Tier 3 — Industry reporting

Specialist energy, infrastructure and financial media.

### Tier 4 — Commentary

Use only as leads or context unless independently verified.

## Claim Handling

Each material claim should be classifiable as:

- FACT
- INFERENCE
- ASSUMPTION
- UNKNOWN

Record:
- source
- publication or effective date
- relevant unit
- project stage where applicable
- confidence or unresolved contradiction where material

## Project-Maturity Discipline

Do not treat all announced megawatts equally.

Distinguish, where evidence permits:

- concept
- early development
- environmental / planning process
- permitted
- grid-qualified
- procurement
- FID / committed
- construction
- operational
- suspended / cancelled

The classification should follow evidence, not promotional language.

## Research Questions

Focus on questions that can change the entry decision:

1. Which projects are sufficiently mature to matter?
2. What is the realistic procurement path?
3. What qualifications are required?
4. What grid or permitting constraints can move timing?
5. What execution conditions affect foundations and erection?
6. What logistics and weather conditions create material cost or schedule risk?
7. What contract structures are likely?
8. What economics would make participation attractive?
9. What local presence, partners or certifications may be required?
10. Which unknowns must be resolved before bidding?

## Contradictions

When credible sources disagree:

1. preserve both claims
2. compare dates and definitions
3. prefer the more authoritative primary record where justified
4. mark the issue unresolved if evidence remains insufficient

Do not average incompatible facts.

## Stop Rule

Continue research only while new evidence has a reasonable chance of:

- changing GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO
- reducing a material risk
- resolving a blocking uncertainty
- changing resource allocation

Otherwise stop.
