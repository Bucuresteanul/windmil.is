# Evidence Register

**Project:** windmil.is  
**Scope:** Icelandic wind-energy market entry  
**Public evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-08

This register contains only public evidence that can materially affect the market-entry decision.

The purpose is not to collect everything. It is to preserve the facts, inferences and unknowns that can change **GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO**.

---

## E-001 — Iceland's first wind farm has entered operation

**Type:** FACT  
**Date of source:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** Landsvirkjun  
**Source link:** https://www.landsvirkjun.com/news/icelands-first-wind-farm-commences-operations

Landsvirkjun states that Vaðalda Wind Farm was commissioned on 15 September 2026 and began sending electricity into Landsnet's transmission system.

**Decision relevance:** Iceland has moved from wind-project development into actual utility-scale wind operation.

**What this does not prove:** It does not establish that additional primary construction packages remain open to a new entrant.

---

## E-002 — Grid connection infrastructure for the first phase is operational

**Type:** FACT  
**Date of source:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** Landsnet  
**Source link:** https://www.landsnet.is/utgafa-og-samskipti/frettir/frett/?documentid=31ff52c4-46e3-4d70-8667-40b6e90a3f15

Landsnet states that the first phase of Vaðölduver was connected to the transmission system through a new 220 kV substation at Ferjufit. Landsnet also states that the infrastructure was designed with possible expansion in mind.

**Decision relevance:** Grid integration of utility-scale wind is no longer theoretical in Iceland.

**Unknown:** Whether this specific infrastructure creates practical capacity for additional wind projects beyond the current Vaðalda development requires project- and grid-specific evidence.

## E-003 — The principal wind-turbine package at Vaðalda is already awarded

**Type:** FACT  
**Date of source:** 2024-11-28  
**Source:** Landsvirkjun  
**Source link:** https://www.landsvirkjun.com/news/enercon-to-supply-wind-turbines

Landsvirkjun awarded Enercon the contract covering design, manufacture, transportation, installation and testing of 28 wind turbines. The published contract value was approximately EUR 140 million / just over ISK 20 billion. A service agreement for at least 15 years is to follow completion.

Landsvirkjun stated that the first 14 turbines were planned for 2026 and the second half for 2027.

**Decision relevance:** The main turbine-supply and turbine-installation package for Vaðalda is not an open greenfield opportunity for a new primary bidder.

**Important boundary:** This does not establish whether subcontracting, ancillary works or future-project opportunities are open.

## E-004 — The principal foundations and civil-works package at Vaðalda is already awarded

**Type:** FACT  
**Date of source:** 2025-05-02  
**Source:** Landsvirkjun  
**Source link:** https://www.landsvirkjun.com/news/construction-at-vadolduver-starts

Landsvirkjun states that Ístak ehf. was awarded an approximately USD 53 million construction package after seven tenders.

The scope includes:

- reinforced-concrete foundations for 28 wind turbines
- crane bases
- groundwork
- an approximately 1,000 m² substation building
- utility infrastructure
- water supply
- drainage
- parking and related works

The published execution period was May 2025 through summer 2027.

**Decision relevance:** The main Vaðalda foundation and civil-works package is already contracted.

**Initial inference:** windmil.is should not model the Vaðalda main foundations package as an available primary-contract opportunity.

## E-005 — Landsvirkjun uses formal tender and qualification channels

**Type:** FACT  
**Source:** Landsvirkjun Procurement  
**Accessed:** 2026-10-08  
**Source link:** https://www.landsvirkjun.com/procurement/tenders

Landsvirkjun publishes tender openings and award results. Its published record states that the 2024 Vaðalda wind-turbine-generator procurement was tendered through the Achilles qualification system.

**Decision relevance:** Market entry requires understanding formal procurement and prequalification, not only identifying projects.

**Unknown:** The exact qualification route for the packages most relevant to windmil.is must be verified package by package.

---

## E-006 — Current regulatory administration gives wind projects a defined licensing path

**Type:** FACT  
**Source:** Orkustofnun  
**Accessed:** 2026-10-08  
**Source link:** https://orkustofnun.is/en/natural_resources/wind

Orkustofnun states that wind farms with installed capacity of 10 MW or more are required to be part of Iceland's framework planning process. It also states that the authority administers wind-energy utilisation for electricity production and grants operational licences under the Electricity Act.

**Decision relevance:** Regulatory readiness is a gating issue for project maturity and timing.

**Unknown:** Project-specific planning, environmental, municipal and construction requirements must still be checked individually.

---

## E-007 — Vaðalda remains a phased build rather than a completed 120 MW reference project

**Type:** FACT ABOUT PUBLISHED PLAN  
**Date of source:** 2026-05-28  
**Source:** Landsvirkjun  
**Source link:** https://www.landsvirkjun.com/news/landsvirkjun-issues-usd-220-million-green-bonds

Landsvirkjun described Vaðalda as a 28-turbine, 120 MW project. At that date it expected the first 14 turbines to enter operation in autumn 2026 and the remaining turbines one year later, with full operation before the end of 2027.

The first phase was subsequently commissioned in September 2026, as recorded in E-001.

**Decision relevance:** The project provides current construction and operating evidence, but should not yet be described as a fully completed 120 MW wind farm unless a later source confirms completion.

# First Bounded Finding

## FINDING-001 — Vaðalda validates the market but is not the primary entry target

**Classification:** INFERENCE  
**Confidence:** High, based on currently identified primary sources.

The Vaðalda project proves that Iceland has entered utility-scale wind construction and operation.

However:

- the turbine package is already awarded to Enercon
- the principal foundations and civil-works package is already awarded to Ístak
- the first phase is already operating
- remaining construction is already under established contracts

Therefore the first market-entry question should shift from:

> Can windmil.is enter through the main Vaðalda packages?

to:

> Which future Icelandic wind projects, subcontracting channels or yet-unawarded packages could provide an executable entry path?

This is a change in targeting, not a NO-GO decision on Iceland.

# Highest-Value Unknowns

The next research should focus only on unknowns capable of changing the entry decision:

1. Which wind projects after Vaðalda are sufficiently mature to create work within the relevant time horizon?
2. Which foundation, civil, erection or related packages remain unawarded?
3. Which owners, developers, OEMs and main contractors control access to those packages?
4. What Achilles, local registration, HSE, financial, insurance and technical qualifications are required?
5. Can a foreign specialist contractor enter directly, or is an Icelandic partner / entity materially advantageous or required?
6. What subcontracting opportunities remain around Vaðalda before summer 2027, if any?
7. What realistic package size, margin and mobilization cost would justify entry?
