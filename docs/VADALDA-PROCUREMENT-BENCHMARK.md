# Vaðalda Procurement Benchmark

**Project:** Vaðalda / Vaðölduver  
**Purpose:** Extract reusable market-entry evidence from Iceland's first utility-scale wind procurement  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-08

This is a procurement benchmark, not a cost estimate for future projects.

---

## Why Vaðalda Matters

Vaðalda is already too advanced to be treated as windmil.is's primary greenfield entry target.

Its value now is different:

**it shows how a real Icelandic utility-scale wind project was packaged, tendered, evaluated and awarded.**

That is directly useful for future entry strategy.

---

## Package Map

### BUF30 — Wind Turbine Generator

**Buyer:** Landsvirkjun  
**Procurement route:** Achilles qualification system  
**Award:** Enercon GmbH  
**Published contract scope:** design, manufacture, transport, installation and testing of 28 turbines  
**Published contract value:** approximately EUR 140 million

### Entry lesson

Large OEM / turbine packages can use prequalification rather than a simple open civil tender.

Do not assume the same procurement route applies to foundations.

---

### BUF05 — Road Construction

**Tender year:** 2024  
**Bids published:** 5  
**Winner:** Borgarverk ehf.  
**Published award basis:** price and quality

Published bids included a separate **carbon cost** alongside price.

### Entry lesson

Carbon evaluation is not merely corporate messaging. It has appeared explicitly in Landsvirkjun construction tender comparison.

A future civil bid should be able to quantify and defend logistics, materials and construction emissions.

---

### BUF10 — Structures

**Tender no.:** 2025-02  
**Procedure:** open construction tender  
**Scope included:**

- reinforced-concrete foundations for 28 wind turbines
- crane hardstands / bases
- groundwork
- utility works
- approximately 1,000 m² site / substation building
- connection-related works
- gravel areas and associated civil infrastructure

**Published cost estimate:** 8,974,292,057 ISK including VAT  
**Bids received:** 7  
**Winner:** Ístak hf.  
**Published contract award value:** 5,498,981,850 ISK excluding VAT  
**Contract date in award notice:** 2025-04-25  
**Published execution:** 2025-04-28 to 2027-08-31 in procurement notice

The Landsvirkjun opening record listed these bidder structures:

1. Ístak hf.
2. K16 ehf. + MFB GmbH
3. Íslenskir aðalverktakar hf.
4. Eykt ehf. + Þjótandi ehf. + MFB Bau GmbH
5. Borgarverk ehf. + Fortis ehf.
6. ÞG verktakar ehf. + Suðurverk ehf.
7. LNS AS

### Entry lesson 1 — Foreign participation is real

The bidder field was not limited to standalone Icelandic contractors. The published list includes foreign corporate forms and cross-company consortium structures.

Current Icelandic AOSH records also list MFB GmbH and Leonhard Nilsen & Sønner AS among registered foreign service providers.

This is evidence that a foreign-service-provider route is operational in the Icelandic market.

**Boundary:** it does not establish the exact legal or commercial structure required for a future windmil.is bid.

### Entry lesson 2 — Consortium is a credible route

Several BUF10 bids were submitted by combinations of companies.

That makes the following a credible strategic option for future investigation:

**foreign specialist capability + Icelandic/local or Nordic delivery structure**

This is an observed market pattern, not yet a recommendation to select any named partner.

### Entry lesson 3 — Carbon can affect competitiveness

The opening report published both tender cost and carbon cost for BUF10.

Therefore future bid economics should not optimize only nominal construction price.

A credible bid model should be able to address:

- concrete and reinforcement footprint
- transport distance
- heavy equipment mobilisation
- local sourcing
- fuel / energy assumptions
- construction method
- carbon-accounting methodology required by the buyer

---

## Important Cost Warning

Do **not** divide the BUF10 contract value by 28 and call the result “foundation cost per turbine.”

The package includes materially more than turbine foundations.

Any normalized benchmark must separate:

- foundation concrete / reinforcement
- excavation and backfill
- crane hardstands
- roads / gravel areas where applicable
- utility infrastructure
- buildings
- connection works
- temporary works
- site preliminaries
- logistics
- risk and escalation

Until the bill of quantities or equivalent package breakdown is available, the aggregate contract is only a package-level benchmark.

---

## Reusable Procurement Pattern

For future Icelandic wind projects, screen for these package families separately:

1. **Development / engineering**
2. **Roads and early enabling works**
3. **Turbine supply / OEM**
4. **Foundations and civil structures**
5. **Electrical collection system**
6. **Grid / substation interface**
7. **Heavy transport**
8. **Crane / erection**
9. **Construction supervision**
10. **Operations and service**

A project can be commercially “closed” in one package and still open in another.

# Decision Consequence

Vaðalda changes the entry hypothesis from:

> A foreign specialist probably needs to become a fully local contractor before it can compete.

to the more precise hypothesis:

> Foreign participation and consortium structures are demonstrably possible, but the optimal legal, qualification and partner structure is package-specific.

That is a materially narrower and more testable question.
