# Qualification & Access Gate

**Project:** windmil.is  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-08  
**Purpose:** Define what must be understood before a foreign specialist contractor can responsibly treat Icelandic wind work as bid-ready.

This is a decision-support document, not legal, tax or employment advice.

---

## Executive Position

There is no evidence that “having relevant construction experience” is sufficient by itself to enter Icelandic wind infrastructure.

A credible entry path combines:

**commercial access + legal operating route + labour compliance + tax/VAT setup + licensed responsibilities + machinery rights + HSE readiness + customer-specific qualification**

These gates should be worked in parallel with project screening.

---

## Gate A — Procurement Access

### Observed market evidence

Vaðalda's principal structures package provides a useful precedent: Landsvirkjun published seven bids, including consortium structures and foreign corporate participants. Current AOSH records separately show foreign construction companies such as MFB GmbH and Leonhard Nilsen & Sønner AS registered as foreign service providers in Iceland.

This demonstrates that foreign participation is practical, while leaving the optimal structure for any future package open.

See [Vaðalda Procurement Benchmark](./VADALDA-PROCUREMENT-BENCHMARK.md).



### Landsvirkjun

Landsvirkjun states that:

- all open procurement processes are published through its tender platform
- suppliers must register and sign in to participate
- processes can include public tenders and market surveys
- it follows the Utilities Procurement Directive 2014/25/EU and Icelandic procurement rules
- some procurements use qualification systems such as Achilles

The Vaðalda wind-turbine-generator package was tendered through Achilles.

The published Vaðalda civil/foundation package was competitively tendered and attracted seven bids.

### Action condition

For any Landsvirkjun opportunity, confirm:

- exact procurement portal
- qualification system
- deadline
- turnover / financial requirements
- reference-project requirements
- insurance / bond requirements
- HSE requirements
- consortium / subcontract rules
- award criteria
- carbon / environmental evaluation

Do not generalise one tender's qualification criteria to another.

**Sources**
- https://www.landsvirkjun.com/procurement
- https://www.landsvirkjun.com/procurement/tenders

## Gate B — Foreign Service Provider Route

A company established in an EEA/EFTA state can temporarily post employees to Iceland to provide services.

For operations lasting more than ten working days, the foreign service provider must submit required information to the Administration of Occupational Safety and Health.

Posted employees remain entitled to Icelandic minimum employment protections and applicable collective-agreement terms.

### Commercial implication

A temporary cross-border service route exists.

That does **not** mean every major construction contract can or should be executed without an Icelandic establishment.

## Gate C — Chain Liability

In construction and civil engineering, Icelandic user undertakings have verification duties concerning foreign service providers and may face chain liability for unpaid employee wage components.

### Commercial implication

A main contractor or customer has a direct reason to scrutinise:

- employment contracts
- working-time records
- payslips
- actual payment
- collective-agreement compliance
- subcontractor chain

A foreign entrant should arrive with evidence-ready payroll and workforce compliance.

**Sources**
- https://island.is/en/p/foreign-service-providers-and-posted-workers/user-undertaking
- https://island.is/en/p/foreign-service-providers-and-posted-workers/exemptions-from-chain-liability-in-construction-and-civil-engineering

## Gate D — Tax, VAT and Corporate Structure

Iceland Revenue and Customs states that services performed in Iceland by non-resident businesses can create Icelandic tax obligations.

Foreign taxable persons can also have Icelandic VAT obligations. Where there is no Icelandic permanent establishment, VAT rules may require an Iceland-domiciled representative.

A registered Icelandic branch is one available operating structure. The tax authority publishes a dedicated branch-registration process and indicates a typical processing time of approximately two to three weeks for complete applications.

### Decision still open

Choose between, for example:

- temporary foreign service-provider model
- Icelandic branch
- Icelandic subsidiary
- consortium / subcontracting structure

only after the target package and tax/legal advice are known.

**Sources**
- https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/contractors/
- https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/value-added-tax/
- https://www.skatturinn.is/english/company-registration/register-a-company/registration-of-a-foreign-branch/

## Gate E — Regulated Construction Responsibility

Iceland has licensed construction-management and certified-trade roles.

HMS states that construction managers administer and carry responsibility for construction work, with different classes of operating licence depending on the structure and scope.

Quality-control systems are part of the licensing framework for responsible construction roles.

### Important boundary

This does not prove that every employee of a foreign civil contractor needs an Icelandic construction-manager licence.

It means that the project's legally responsible roles and sign-off architecture must be identified before bidding.

**Sources**
- https://island.is/en/construction-managers-license
- https://island.is/en/quality-control-system
- https://island.is/en/o/hms/licences-and-certification

## Gate F — Machinery and Lifting Rights

Iceland requires work-machine rights for categories including cranes, large excavators, wheel loaders, bulldozers, graders and other machinery.

The Administration of Occupational Safety and Health states that an EEA-issued heavy-machinery licence must be recognised in Iceland.

### Relevance to wind foundations / erection

This can affect:

- crane crews
- earthworks crews
- lifting operations
- mobilisation lead time
- subcontractor selection

**Source**
https://island.is/en/work-machine-rights

## Gate G — Customer HSE

For Landsvirkjun worksites, contractor requirements include:

- project/task risk assessment
- contractor safety training
- PPE
- incident and near-miss reporting
- work permits in relevant operational areas
- emergency / first-aid arrangements
- job-specific licences and competence

Landsvirkjun's current basic induction combines an SSG Iceland course and a Landsvirkjun-specific course. The published training is valid for three years.

**Sources**
- https://www.landsvirkjun.com/safety/safety-for-contractors
- https://www.landsvirkjun.com/safety-and-environmental-training

# Readiness Model

## R0 — Unknown

No package-specific requirements collected.

## R1 — Legally understood

Foreign-service, tax, VAT and regulated-role pathways mapped.

## R2 — Administratively preparable

Required registrations, representatives, licence recognition and HSE evidence can be completed within the likely bid / mobilisation window.

## R3 — Prequalification-ready

Reference projects, financial evidence, HSE records, insurance and customer-specific documentation are assembled.

## R4 — Bid-ready

A specific opportunity has:
- confirmed scope
- confirmed buyer / contracting route
- satisfied prequalification
- execution plan
- bounded economics
- acceptable risk

windmil.is should not describe itself as bid-ready before R4 is evidenced.

# Current Assessment

**Qualification state:** R1 — partially established at public-information level.

What remains unresolved:

- package-specific prequalification
- required insurance and bonding
- whether the target buyer accepts a foreign-service-provider structure
- exact Icelandic responsible-role configuration for wind-foundation / erection packages
- recognition pathway for specific personnel and machinery operators
- project-specific collective agreement / payroll model
- bank / tax / VAT operating mechanics
- customer reference-project thresholds

These are now bounded questions rather than an undefined “local requirements” problem.
