# Decision Framework

## Strategic Decision

Should windmil.is commit further resources to pursuing Icelandic wind-energy infrastructure opportunities?

## Decision Outcomes

### GO

Use only when evidence supports a credible route to executable opportunity and the major qualification, economics, delivery and risk questions are sufficiently bounded.

### CONDITIONAL GO

Use when the opportunity appears credible but one or more material dependencies must be resolved before commitment.

Each condition must be explicit and testable.

### NO-GO

Use when the expected value, access path, timing, qualification burden or risk does not justify further commitment.

NO-GO is not failure. It is a valid capital- and attention-preservation decision.

## Core Gates

### Gate 1 — Market Reality

Is there a sufficiently mature project pipeline to justify attention now?

### Gate 2 — Access

Can the relevant work packages actually be competed for or accessed?

### Gate 3 — Qualification

Can required legal, technical, financial, safety and local requirements be met within a defensible timeframe and cost?

### Gate 4 — Delivery

Can foundations, erection, logistics and site execution be delivered under Icelandic conditions with acceptable risk?

### Gate 5 — Economics

Can expected margin and strategic value justify mobilization, overhead and risk?

### Gate 6 — Risk

Are regulatory, contractual, political, reputational and counterparty risks acceptable or mitigable?

## Decision Record

A final recommendation should state:

- outcome
- date
- evidence cutoff
- strongest evidence for
- strongest evidence against
- blocking unknowns
- required conditions
- confidence
- next action
- stop condition

## Resource Rule

Do not commit additional effort because substantial effort has already been spent.

Evaluate the next unit of time, attention and capital on expected future value.
